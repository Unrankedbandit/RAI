Headline findings

Two are threshold criteria (the project cannot proceed as described); three are material degradations. The rubric should score these as separate tiers.

Threshold Zoning kill switch

Solano's 2015 Solar Ordinance (ZT 12-04) prohibits commercial solar in all ag zoning districts; the Aug 2025 BESS ordinance bans batteries on ag land. A local use permit is impossible. Viable path: CEC AB 205 Opt-In Certification (270-day state decision, preempts local zoning) — the route NextEra's Corby BESS is using in Solano now (Docket 24-OPT-05).

Threshold Tax credit cliff

Under OBBBA (Jul 2025), COD July 2028 gets $0 in ITC/PTC unless beginning-of-construction was documented by July 4, 2026 — already passed. The BOC evidence package is the single biggest go/no-go item in the diligence.

Critical CAPEX confirmed impossible

Real benchmarks: $1.43–1.56/Wac installed (LBNL / NREL). The demo's $186M / 250 MW = $0.74/W, roughly half of market — worse once BESS is included. The seeded red flag is validated by reality.

Critical Transformers worse than demo says

Demo flags 62 weeks; the real 2025–26 norm for large power transformers/GSUs is 128–144 weeks (~2.5–2.8 yrs). For COD July 2028, orders had to be placed by mid-2025.

Critical Swainson's hawk is the schedule

Solano is core breeding habitat. Surveys follow a strict seasonal protocol with a blackout window Apr 21 – Jun 10 — miss the season, lose a year. One active nest can seasonally sterilize 125–500 acres.

The 11 components — benchmarks & red-flag checklists

The rubric's knowledge base, component by component. Expand to review.

1 State law (California) Path-defining
  • CEQA: a 1,420-ac farmland conversion always requires a full EIR — 12–24 mo to certify; 18–30 mo total entitlement; +1–3 yrs if litigated (~43% plaintiff win rate vs EIRs).
  • CEC Opt-In (AB 205/209): eligible = solar ≥50 MW + storage ≥200 MWh; CEC decides within 270 days; certificate in lieu of all local permits. Requires certified CBA + prevailing wage + skilled-and-trained workforce.
  • Williamson Act: solar is an incompatible use; cancellation at 12.5% of unrestricted FMV (25% FSZ); solar-use easement at 6.25% only for impaired farmland.
  • SB 149/1420: Governor-certified projects get CEQA suits resolved in ~270 days (sunsets 2034).
  • WA contract status on every parcel
  • CEQA doc assumed to be full EIR
  • Permitting path explicitly chosen (Opt-In vs local — local is prohibited)
  • Opt-In labor/CBA prerequisites certified
  • DAC screen re-run on CalEnviroScreen 5.0 (4.0 data is stale)
2 Federal law
  • NEPA only with a federal nexus (CWA §404, federal funding/ROW). In flux: CEQ rescinded all NEPA regs (Jan 8, 2026); Seven County narrowed scope.
  • ESA §7: formal consultation up to 135 days. Exposure: vernal pool fairy shrimp, CA tiger salamander, giant garter snake, Delta smelt.
  • MBTA: strict liability, no incidental take permit — compliance = avoidance. BGEPA: golden eagles in the Montezuma Hills; nest searches to 1 mile.
  • WOTUS post-Sackett: isolated seasonal wetlands likely non-jurisdictional federally — but CA's State Wetland Procedures backstop them via 401/WDRs.
  • IRA labor hooks: prevailing wage + apprenticeship (15% ratio) multiplies credits 5×.
  • Jurisdictional delineation (AJD) mapping federal vs state-only waters
  • Federal nexus identified (or ruled out)
  • Raptor/eagle survey coverage ≥0.5 mi / ≥1 mi, two seasons
  • Vernal pool branchiopod wet+dry season protocol surveys
  • PWA compliance plan + FEOC supply-chain attestations
3 Land permitting (Solano County) Prohibited locally
  • Commercial solar prohibited in all ag districts (2015 Ordinance ZT 12-04). BESS: industrial/manufacturing only (Ord. 2025-1864, Aug 26, 2025).
  • Local route = General Plan + zoning text amendment: 18–36 mo with material denial risk (ag-protectionist county; California Forever politics).
  • Opt-In route: CEC 270-day clock; county-opposition risk documented (Corby docket as template).
  • Construction stack: grading permit (Ch. 31), fire-marshal BESS sign-off (NFPA 855 / UL 9540A), Construction General Permit (Order 2022-0057-DWQ) with SWPPP in SMARTS, CDFW §1602 LSAA for drainage ditches.
  • Zoning district of every parcel confirmed + prohibition screen
  • If Opt-In: ≥50 MW PV + ≥200 MWh BESS verified, pre-filing meeting done
  • BESS safety stack complete
  • CGP/SWPPP/1602 in permit matrix
  • Permit sequence reconciled with environmental milestones (early grading before review = real CEQA defect)
4 Land use & zoning
  • Exclusive Agriculture districts (A-20/40/80/160); General Plan (2008, voter-ratified Measure T) directs growth to cities.
  • Mitigation norms: ag conservation easements at 1:1 (up to 2:1 on prime), decommissioning bond ~100% of removal cost.
  • Travis AFB: ALUC review; 2024 LUCP added Low Altitude Maneuvering Zone, glare-analysis (ForgeSolar/SGHAT), wildlife-attractant criteria for ponds; overruling ALUC takes a 2/3 vote.
  • Overlays: Suisun Marsh MDP, Delta Primary Zone. Land economics: leases ~$935–1,600/ac/yr; purchase $15–40k/ac.
  • Parcel-by-parcel zoning + FMMP tally
  • Ag mitigation package (easement ratio, conservancy partner)
  • Decommissioning financial assurance
  • Travis AFB influence-area screen + glare study + pond review
  • Marsh/Delta screen
  • WA × zoning double-blockage cross-check
5 Ecology & EPA Schedule-driving

See the seasonal calendar above. Other water items: NWP 51 for solar (≤0.5-ac loss), 401/WDR via RWQCB Region 5S, Solano Subbasin GSP (approved Jan 2024) — construction wells need GSA coordination; construction water must be a named, contracted source (the 82 vs 50 acre-ft gap is realistic — SWP allocations hit 0–5% in recent droughts). Expect a 5–15% developable-acreage haircut from bio/water constraints (matches the 46-acre raptor-buffer conflict).

  • Full-season protocol surveys ≤2 yrs old
  • §2081 ITP need determined with CDFW
  • BUOW candidacy addressed
  • CTS 2-season program scoped or concurrence documented
  • GGS assume-present analysis
  • AJD verified + state waters mapped · CGP risk level determined
  • Named construction-water source
  • Seasonal constraint register in the integrated schedule
6 Community stakeholders
  • Politicized county: Orderly Growth Initiative, California Forever controversy, actual BESS moratorium (Jan 2024) → permanent BESS ordinance (Aug 2025) after fire-safety backlash.
  • AB 52 tribal consultation must conclude before EIR certification (fatal defect if not). Tribes: Yocha Dehe Wintun Nation, Kletsel Dehe, Cachil Dehe. NAHC Sacred Lands File search required.
  • CBA anchors: Darden (1,150 MW) = $2M; Jacumba (~90 MW) = $4M. Screen: $0.5–5M one-time; <$1,000/MW is below-market. A CBA is mandatory for the CEC Opt-In path.
  • Property values (LBNL 2023): −2.3% within 0.25 mi, none beyond 1 mi.
  • EJ: CalEnviroScreen 5.0 final Jul 1, 2026 — screens run on 4.0 are a defect.
  • AB 52 notice/consultation timeline
  • CBA ≥ regional benchmarks with community-defined allocation
  • Truck-routing protocol agreed before hearings (fallback-route conflict = classic appeal trigger)
  • Drainage acknowledgments from adjacent ag operators
  • Opposition map (Farm Bureau, Solano Land Trust, Vaca-Dixon BESS opponents)
  • CES 5.0 EJ screen · local-hire/PLA commitment

  • FEOC/MACR attestations · domestic content ≥55% BOM for +10 pts
  • Contingency ≥5% of EPC

8 Interconnection (CAISO)
  • Queue >500 GW / ~1,000 projects; only ~20% ever reach COD; typical IR→COD ~5 yrs.
  • Reformed process (FERC Oct 2024): zonal approach, intake capped at 150% of available capacity per zone; scored intake (Commercial Interest 30% / Viability 35% / System Need 35%).
  • Deliverability: FCDS = can sell Resource Adequacy; Energy-Only = materially lower revenue. TPD priority: executed PPA first.
  • Material modifications that blow up a queue position: POI change, MW upsize, fuel-type change, storage additions.
  • Network upgrade cost shock is the #1 dropout driver (~30% post-screen attrition); CAISO transmission projects average +6.1 yrs delay.
  • Queue position + cluster phase verified in CAISO public queue
  • Deliverable vs Merchant zone for the POI
  • Phase II study complete with upgrade cost + 30–50% contingency
  • FCDS confirmed in writing · IFS postings current
  • Material-modification audit
  • Site control incl. gen-tie corridor (Parcel 14 sits on the collector route)
  • No COD claim <24 months without executed studies
9 Grid integration
  • Solar is now California's #1 electricity source (2025); duck curve = 10–17 GW evening ramp.
  • Standalone solar NQC ≈10% and ineligible for the new 6 GW CPUC procurement; 4-hr storage ≈90–95% NQC.
  • Curtailment: 3.4 TWh in 2024 (+29% YoY, 93% solar); negative midday prices routine.
  • Battery gross revenue fell $115 → $52/kW-yr (2022→2025). Slice-of-Day RA framework effective 2025.
  • Storage pairing ≥4 hr · FCDS for RA revenue
  • Curtailment assumption ≥10–13% with nodal (not flat) capture rates
  • Merchant assumptions ≤$52/kW-yr battery benchmark
  • POI-level constraint check · grid-charging treatment in RA counting
10 Buyers & demand Bankability gap
  • Buyers: PG&E (12.7 GW data-center pipeline — volatile), MCE is the CCA serving Solano, neighboring CCAs, Direct Access ESPs, corporate buyers.
  • CPUC IRP (Feb 2026): 6,000 MW NQC new procurement (2 GW each by 2030/31/32); 750 MW must be ≥8-hr storage.
  • PPA prices: Q3 2025 $59.77 → Q1 2026 $64.49/MWh (+13% YoY).
  • Bankability: ≥80% of P50 executed at close; executed ≠ LOI; single counterparty >50% flagged; CCA offtake → check credit support.
  • Executed PPA MW vs LOI/term-sheet MW (150 firm vs 200 assumed = exactly this)
  • ≥80% contracted at close · tenor ≥ debt + 2-yr tail
  • RA/NQC sold at current ELCC derates with FCDS to deliver it
  • Counterparty credit/concentration · PPA ≥ $60–65/MWh current index
  • Financing-close vs offtake-execution sequence (Oct close vs Nov contract = classic CP failure)
11 Resource availability & supply chain Hardest gate
  • Resource (Global Solar Atlas @ Rio Vista/Delta Solano): GHI 5.32 kWh/m²/day; PVOUT 1,807 kWh/kWp; single-axis-tracking AC capacity factor ~27–31%. >32% claim = red flag.
  • Acreage: 1,420 ac / 250 MW = 5.7 ac/MW — dense but plausible (6–9 empirical); verify usable acreage after buffers.
  • Physical risks: Delta flood/levee (elev ~10 m), Carquinez-gap wind (tracker stow), expansive soils, wildfire-smoke soiling (−10–30% in bad years).
  • P90/P50 ≈ 0.90–0.94; P90 CFADS must cover ≥1.20x DSCR.
  • Transformer PO executed with ≤Q4 2027 delivery; lead-time confirmation in writing <90 days old
  • Module MSA with FEOC/MACR + UFLPA traceability + tariff reopener
  • DC BOM ≥55% with supplier certs · BESS slot + compliant cells
  • P90 yield report on ≥20-yr satellite data + on-site met · CF claim in 27–31% band
  • Flood/wind/geotech memos · ≥6 months float between major-equipment delivery and COD
