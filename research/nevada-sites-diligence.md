## PARCEL B — "RAI Solar B" · 180 MWac · inside Sloan Canyon NCA, NV
**Verdict: NO-GO / RE-SITE — VALIDATED and strengthened · readiness ≈ 38/100 (fatal flaw gates)**
The framework confirmed the memo's fatal-flaw conclusion under primary legal sources — and found it's *understated*.

### The legal wall (all real, all current)
| Authority | Effect |
|---|---|
| **PL 107-282 (2002), 16 U.S.C. §460qqq–3(c)** | BLM "shall allow **only such uses** that further the purpose" of the NCA (conservation of cultural/archaeological/scenic resources) — industrial solar fails the test |
| §460qqq–3(e) | All NCA land **withdrawn from entry/appropriation** under public land laws, mining, mineral/geothermal leasing |
| **2024 Western Solar Plan ROD** (89 FR 105623) | Excludes **~131M acres** — dossier's figure exactly right; NCAs categorically named; **variance lands eliminated as a category** — no escape hatch; ROD *amended the Sloan Canyon NCA RMP itself* |
| FLPMA §202 / 43 CFR 1610 | ROW applications must conform to the governing RMP → rejected at screening |
| **North McCullough Wilderness (14,790 ac, inside NCA)** | Wilderness Act §4(c) absolute bar |
| **PL 119-91 (May 19, 2026) — FACT-CHECKED: REAL** | "Sloan Canyon Conservation and Lateral Pipeline Act" (H.R. 972, Rep. Titus): NCA expanded **48,438 → 57,728 acres (+19.2%)**; the only development authorized is a congressionally-mandated **water** pipeline ROW, expressly barred from wilderness. GovInfo-verified |

### Cross-examination catch (the framework's signature)
- Developer's site-control map labels the tract **"Eldorado East Expansion"** assuming solar is allowed — the geospatial/legal cross-check places the footprint inside the NCA (possibly *deeper* inside after the May 2026 expansion).
- Financial workbook still carries **$4.8M pre-NTP spend + Q4 2029 COD copied from Parcel A** — model assumes a developable site; land evidence says otherwise. Stop discretionary spend; preserve transferable studies.

### Baseline check (why land status is the SOLE decisive factor — confirmed)
Irradiance/market/transmission genuinely comparable across the boundary (Mojave GHI ~5.7–6.2; NV RPS 50% by 2030; Eldorado/Mead 500-kV hub adjacent). Real precedents of protected-land gates killing/resizing projects: Ivanpah (440→392 MW, tortoise), Amargosa SEZ (31,625→8,479 ac), Esmeralda Seven (~6 GW, BLM review canceled Oct 2025).

### Re-site recommendation (real, ranked)
1. **Eldorado Valley / Boulder City basin** — private/city or conforming land next to Site A; keeps design basis + 500-kV hub
2. **Dry Lake SEZ + Dry Lake East DLA** (Clark Co.) — 5,717 developable acres, 500-kV corridors in-zone, ROW precedent (Aug 2024)
3. **Amargosa Valley SEZ** (Nye Co.) — 8,479 ac; weak 138-kV today, improves with Greenlink West
4. Dry Lake Valley North SEZ (25,069 ac) — acreage rich, gen-tie poor
