# Project Red Flag — Solar Due-Diligence Component Research
**Subject project:** Solar Alpha — 250 MW solar-plus-storage, ~1,420 ac, Solano County, CA
**Compiled:** August 14, 2026 · Compiled from 4 parallel web-research agents (sources cited per section)
**Purpose:** knowledge base for the Red Flag scoring rubric — each component has real-world benchmarks + red-flag indicators the app can check uploaded dossiers against.

---

## HEADLINE FINDINGS (read these first)

1. **🔴 ZONING KILL SWITCH.** Solano County's 2015 Solar Ordinance (ZT 12-04) **prohibits commercial solar in ALL agricultural zoning districts**, and the Aug 2025 BESS Ordinance (2025-1864) prohibits front-of-meter batteries on ag land too. A 250 MW project on 1,420 acres of farmland **cannot get a local use permit**. The viable path is the **CEC AB 205 Opt-In Certification** (solar ≥ 50 MW + storage ≥ 200 MWh eligible; 270-day state decision; preempts local zoning) — the exact route NextEra's Corby BESS is using in Solano right now (Docket 24-OPT-05). This is a **threshold/kill criterion**, not a schedule risk.
2. **🔴 TAX CREDIT CLIFF.** Under OBBBA (July 2025), a project with **COD July 2028 gets $0 in ITC/PTC** unless it documented **beginning-of-construction by July 4, 2026** — a deadline that has *already passed* as of today. The BOC evidence package is the single biggest go/no-go item in the whole diligence.
3. **🔴 CAPEX CONFIRMED IMPOSSIBLE.** Real benchmarks: $1.43–1.56/Wac installed (LBNL/NREL). The demo's $186M / 250 MW = **$0.74/W — roughly half of market**, and worse once BESS is included. The synthetic red flag is validated by reality.
4. **🔴 TRANSFORMERS ARE WORSE THAN THE DEMO SAYS.** Demo flags 62 weeks; the real 2025–26 market norm for large power transformers/GSUs is **128–144 weeks (~2.5–2.8 years)**. For COD July 2028, orders had to be placed by mid-2025.
5. **🔴 SWAINSON'S HAWK IS THE SCHEDULE.** Solano County is core breeding habitat for this state-threatened raptor. Surveys follow a strict seasonal protocol with a **blackout window (Apr 21–Jun 10)** — miss the season, lose a year. One active nest can seasonally sterilize 125–500 acres.

## COMPONENT 1 — STATE LAW (California)

**Key facts & benchmarks:**
- **CEQA:** a 1,420-ac farmland conversion always requires a full **EIR** — 12–24 months to certify; 18–30 months total entitlement; +1–3 years if litigated. Renewables are the most-frequently-challenged project type (~43% plaintiff win rate vs EIRs). Farmland impact scored via the **Agricultural LESA Model**; likely "significant and unavoidable" → Statement of Overriding Considerations.
- **CEC Opt-In Certification (AB 205/AB 209, PRC §§25545–25545.9):** eligible = solar ≥ 50 MW, storage ≥ 200 MWh. CEC decides within **270 days** of complete application; certificate is **in lieu of all local permits** (does not preempt Water Board, air districts, or federal permits). **Requires certified community benefits agreement + prevailing wage + skilled-and-trained workforce.**
- **Williamson Act:** commercial solar is an *incompatible use* on contracted land. Exit paths: 9-yr nonrenewal; cancellation at **12.5% of unrestricted FMV** (25% for Farmland Security Zone); solar-use easement at 6.25% (only for *impaired* farmland; reinstated Jan 2023 by SB 1489). Solano County does not accept new WA contracts and is ag-protectionist.
- **SB 149/SB 1420 judicial streamlining:** Governor-certified energy projects get CEQA suits resolved in ~270 days (sunsets 2034).
- Demand driver: SB 100/SB 1020 — 100% zero-carbon by 2045.

**Red-flag checklist:** ☐ WA contract status on every parcel ☐ CEQA doc assumed to be full EIR ☐ permitting path explicitly chosen (Opt-In vs local — local is prohibited) ☐ Opt-In labor/CBA prerequisites certified ☐ DAC screen re-run on CalEnviroScreen **5.0** (released July 1, 2026 — 4.0 data is stale).

## COMPONENT 2 — FEDERAL LAW

**Key facts & benchmarks:**
- **NEPA** applies only with a federal nexus — most commonly a **CWA §404 permit** (wetlands), federal funding, or federal ROW. ⚠ In flux: CEQ rescinded all NEPA regs (final Jan 8, 2026); *Seven County* (May 2025) narrowed scope; agencies rewriting procedures.
- **ESA §7** (if nexus): formal consultation up to 135 days. Solano listed-species exposure: vernal pool fairy shrimp / Conservancy fairy shrimp (Jepson Prairie), CA tiger salamander, giant garter snake, Delta smelt (if hydrologically connected).
- **MBTA:** strict liability, **no incidental take permit exists** — compliance = avoidance (nest surveys, buffers, construction timing). **BGEPA:** golden eagles in the Montezuma Hills; eagle nest searches to **1 mile**.
- **WOTUS post-Sackett:** isolated seasonal wetlands likely non-jurisdictional federally — **but CA's State Wetland Procedures (2020) backstop them** via 401 cert / WDRs. New WOTUS rule proposed Nov 2025, final pending now.
- **IRA labor hooks:** prevailing wage + apprenticeship (15% ratio for 2025+ starts) multiplies credits 5×.

**Red-flag checklist:** ☐ jurisdictional delineation (AJD) mapping federal vs state-only waters ☐ federal nexus identified (or ruled out) ☐ raptor/eagle survey coverage ≥0.5 mi / ≥1 mi, two seasons ☐ vernal pool branchiopod wet+dry season protocol surveys ☐ PWA compliance plan + FEOC supply-chain attestations.

## COMPONENT 3 — LAND PERMITTING (Solano County)

**Key facts & benchmarks:**
- **Commercial solar: prohibited in all ag districts** (2015 Ordinance ZT 12-04). Allowed by use permit only in small commercial/industrial districts — a 1,420-ac project cannot fit. **BESS: industrial/manufacturing districts only** (Ord. 2025-1864, Aug 26, 2025; ERP, fire-zone, and flood-elevation standards).
- Local route = General Plan amendment + zoning text amendment: **18–36 months with material denial risk** (county is ag-protectionist; California Forever politics have inflamed farmland sentiment).
- **Opt-In route:** CEC 270-day clock; county opposition risk documented (Corby docket as template).
- Construction-phase stack: County grading permit (Code Ch. 31), building/electrical, fire-marshal BESS sign-off (NFPA 855 / UL 9540A), **Construction General Permit (Order 2022-0057-DWQ)** — SWPPP by QSD in SMARTS before any ground disturbance, risk level 1/2/3; **CDFW §1602 LSAA** for the site's drainage ditches; encroachment permits.

**Red-flag checklist:** ☐ zoning district of every parcel confirmed + prohibition screen ☐ if Opt-In: ≥50 MW PV + ≥200 MWh BESS verified, pre-filing meeting done, county-comment risk assessed ☐ BESS safety stack complete ☐ CGP/SWPPP/1602 in permit matrix ☐ permit sequence reconciled with environmental milestones (the demo's "early grading before environmental review" flag is a real CEQA defect pattern).

## COMPONENT 4 — LAND USE & ZONING

**Key facts & benchmarks:**
- Zoning: Exclusive Agriculture districts (A-20/40/80/160; A-40/A-80 = prime farmland) — solar prohibited in all. General Plan (2008, voter-ratified Measure T Orderly Growth) directs growth to cities.
- **FMMP Important Farmland** classification drives the CEQA ag analysis; Solano's east county (Dixon Ridge, Maine Prairie, Montezuma Hills margins) is heavily Prime/Statewide Importance.
- Mitigation norms: **ag conservation easements at 1:1** (up to 2:1 on prime), decommissioning bond at **~100% of removal cost**, soil restoration spec.
- **Travis AFB compatibility (Solano-specific):** ALUC review inside Airport Influence Areas; 2024 LUCP amendments added **Low Altitude Maneuvering Zone, solar glare-analysis requirements, wildlife-attractant criteria for stormwater ponds**; accepted glare tool = ForgeSolar/SGHAT; DoD Siting Clearinghouse coordination; overruling ALUC takes a 2/3 vote.
- Overlays: Suisun Marsh (MDP), Delta Primary Zone (Delta Plan consistency) if eastern parcels.
- Land economics: CA solar leases ~$935–1,600/ac/yr → **~$1.3–2.3M/yr for 1,420 ac**; purchase $15–40k/ac.

**Red-flag checklist:** ☐ parcel-by-parcel zoning + FMMP tally ☐ ag mitigation package (easement ratio, conservancy partner) ☐ decommissioning financial assurance ☐ Travis AFB influence-area screen + glare study + pond wildlife-attractant review ☐ Marsh/Delta screen ☐ WA × zoning double-blockage cross-check.

## COMPONENT 6 — COMMUNITY STAKEHOLDERS

**Key facts & benchmarks:**
- Solano is a **politicized county**: Orderly Growth Initiative (voter-ratified), California Forever controversy (~68,000 ac assembled; ballot measure pulled 2024; annexation pivot 2025), and an actual **BESS moratorium (Jan 2024) → permanent BESS ordinance (Aug 2025)** driven by resident fire-safety backlash near the Vaca-Dixon substation.
- **AB 52 tribal consultation:** notice ≤14 days of complete application; consultation must conclude **before EIR certification** (fatal defect if not). Key tribes: **Yocha Dehe Wintun Nation** (Patwin territory expressly includes Solano), Kletsel Dehe, Cachil Dehe. NAHC Sacred Lands File search required.
- **CBA dollar anchors:** Darden (1,150 MW, Fresno) = **$2M** fund (criticized as opaque); Jacumba Valley Ranch (~90 MW, San Diego) = **$4M** fund. Screening range for contested ag siting: **$0.5–5M one-time; treat < $1,000/MW as below-market.** Community-defined allocation (PACT framework) is the emerging standard — and a CBA is *mandatory* for the CEC Opt-In path.
- Property values (LBNL 2023, national): −2.3% within 0.25 mi, −1.5% at 0.25–0.5 mi, none beyond 1 mi; effects concentrated in rural ag areas.
- Ag stakeholder mechanics: documented drainage acknowledgments from adjacent operators (the demo's flag is standard practice); irrigation-district encroachment permits; farmworker displacement belongs in the EJ section.
- **EJ ⚠:** CalEnviroScreen **5.0 final July 1, 2026**; 2026 DAC designation comment period closed **today (Aug 14, 2026)** — any screen run on CES 4.0 is a defect. Solano DACs cluster in Vallejo/Fairfield; rural sites carry pesticide/nitrate indicator context.

**Red-flag checklist:** ☐ AB 52 notice/consultation timeline ☐ CBA ≥ regional benchmarks with community-defined allocation ☐ truck-routing protocol agreed before hearings (the demo's fallback-route conflict is a classic appeal trigger) ☐ drainage acknowledgments from adjacent ag operators ☐ opposition map (Farm Bureau, Solano Land Trust, California-Forweary networks, Vaca-Dixon BESS opponents) ☐ CES 5.0 EJ screen ☐ local-hire/PLA commitment.

## COMPONENT 8 — INTERCONNECTION (CAISO)

**Key facts & benchmarks:**
- Queue reality: CAISO queue **>500 GW / ~1,000 projects**; historically **only ~20% of queued projects ever reach COD**; typical IR→COD now **~5 years** (4–7+ pre-reform).
- Reformed process (FERC-approved Oct 2024): **zonal approach** (Deliverable vs Merchant zones — Merchant upgrades self-funded, **non-reimbursable**), intake capped at **150% of available capacity per zone**, scored intake (Commercial Interest 30% / Viability 35% / System Need 35% — LSE interest points gate everything). Cluster 15: 541 projects / 347 GW applied → **only 145 / 68 GW advanced**.
- **Deliverability:** FCDS = can sell Resource Adequacy; Energy-Only = energy-only nodal revenue (materially lower). TPD allocation priority: executed PPA first — "projects without PPAs nearly always withdraw."
- **Material modifications that blow up a queue position:** POI change, MW upsize, fuel-type change, storage additions that increase service MW; COD beyond 7 years requires PPA proof.
- **Network upgrade cost shock is the #1 dropout driver** (~30% post-screen attrition). CAISO-approved transmission projects average **+6.1 years delay** (+8.4 yrs for deprioritized PG&E projects) — any POI relying on planned transmission carries that distribution.
- Solano POI context: Greater Bay Area local capacity area (Vaca-Dixon corridor); pull CAISO Local Capacity Technical Study + Cluster 15 zonal map for the actual substation.

**Red-flag checklist:** ☐ queue position + cluster phase verified in CAISO public queue ☐ Deliverable vs Merchant zone for the POI ☐ Phase II study complete with upgrade cost + 30–50% contingency ☐ FCDS confirmed in writing ☐ material-modification audit ☐ IFS postings current ☐ site control incl. gen-tie corridor (the demo's Parcel 14 sits on the collector route — this is why it matters) ☐ no COD claim <24 months without executed studies.

## COMPONENT 9 — GRID INTEGRATION

**Key facts & benchmarks:**
- Solar is now **California's #1 electricity source (2025)**; duck curve = 10–17 GW evening ramp. **Standalone solar NQC ≈ 10% of nameplate** (CPUC, 2026) and is **ineligible for the new 6 GW CPUC procurement**; **4-hr storage ≈ 90–95% NQC**. Solar+storage is the only viable configuration in CA.
- Curtailment: 3.4 TWh curtailed 2024 (+29% YoY, 93% solar); **11.5% of potential solar generation curtailed in early 2025**; negative midday prices routine.
- **Merchant revenue compression:** battery gross revenue fell **$115/kW-yr (2022) → $52/kW-yr (2025)**; evening prices >$70/MWh now rare. Underwrite merchant tails to 2025 benchmarks, not 2022 vintage.
- Slice-of-Day RA framework effective 2025; hybrid QC = sum of components capped by POI limit.

**Red-flag checklist:** ☐ storage pairing ≥4 hr ☐ FCDS for RA revenue ☐ curtailment assumption ≥10–13% with nodal (not flat) capture rates ☐ merchant assumptions ≤$52/kW-yr battery benchmark ☐ POI-level constraint check ☐ grid-charging treatment in RA counting.

## COMPONENT 10 — BUYERS & DEMAND

**Key facts & benchmarks:**
- Buyers: PG&E (IOU; 12.7 GW data-center pipeline — volatile), **MCE (Marin Clean Energy) is the CCA serving Solano County**, neighboring CCAs (Ava, SJCE, PCE, SVCE, 3CE), Direct Access ESPs, corporate/data-center buyers.
- Mandate pull: CPUC IRP (Feb 2026) = **6,000 MW NQC new procurement** (2 GW each by 2030/31/32); 750 MW must be ≥8-hr storage; solar+4-hr storage expected to supply most of the rest.
- **PPA prices (LevelTen P25):** Q3 2025 $59.77 → Q1 2026 **$64.49/MWh (+13% YoY)**; CAISO among sharpest increases; hybrid PPAs command premium. LBNL 2023 levelized PPA benchmark: ~$35/MWh (rising since 2021).
- Bankability norms: PPAs 10–20 yr; **≥80% of P50 contracted at financial close**; executed PPA ≠ LOI/MOU/term sheet (only executed counts); single-counterparty >50% of revenue flagged; CCA offtake → check credit support (unrated).
- Storage revenue stack: toll / RA-only contract + arbitrage + ancillary, merchant share stress-tested.

**Red-flag checklist:** ☐ executed PPA MW vs LOI/term-sheet MW (the demo's 150 firm vs 200 assumed is exactly this) ☐ ≥80% contracted at close ☐ tenor ≥ debt + 2-yr tail ☐ RA/NQC sold at current ELCC derates with FCDS to deliver it ☐ counterparty credit/concentration ☐ PPA price ≥ $60–65/MWh current index for hybrid ☐ financing-close vs offtake-execution sequence (demo's Oct close vs Nov contract = classic CP failure).

## COMPONENT 11 — RESOURCE AVAILABILITY & SUPPLY CHAIN

**Solar resource (real data, Global Solar Atlas point query @ 38.25°N, 121.93°W — Rio Vista/Delta Solano, Apr 2026 data):**
- **GHI 5.32 kWh/m²/day** (1,943/yr); DNI 6.8/day; optimal tilt 33°; PVOUT 1,807 kWh/kWp
- Expected single-axis-tracking AC capacity factor **~27–31%** (NREL ATB Class 3). >32% claim = red flag; <25% = question the model.
- **Acreage: 1,420 ac / 250 MW = 5.7 ac/MW** — dense but plausible (LBNL empirical: 6–9 ac/MWac for trackers); verify usable acreage after buffers/setbacks.
- Site physical risks: Delta flood/levee exposure (elev ~10 m), Carquinez-gap wind (tracker stow), expansive/liquefiable soils, wildfire-smoke soiling (weeks of −10–30% insolation in bad smoke years).
- P90/P50 ≈ 0.90–0.94; P90 CFADS must cover ≥1.20x DSCR.
